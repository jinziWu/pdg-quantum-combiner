# Generated PDG 2026 catalog

Source: [official 2026.0 SQLite database](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/api/pdg-2026.0.sqlite) and [Particle Listings index](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/listings/contents_listings.html). Generated by `scripts/build_catalog.py`; do not manually edit `particles.js`. The source URLs, release timestamp, SHA-256 checksums, citation, license, counts, and index-to-record mappings are saved in the accompanying metadata and coverage JSON files.

## Coverage

All 1,170 `pdgparticle` rows are retained. Every `PART` identifier is represented, including the one without a `pdgparticle` mapping. Additional search/topic identifiers make all 458 index entries available for checking. Total: 1,196 entries, of which 1,171 are particle states and 25 are topics. There are no mass, star-rating, name-prefix, or Summary Table membership cuts in the catalog.

The database does not expose a structured star-rating field. `stars` is null, and no star rating is inferred from the presence of a mass, spin, summary, or measurement. This includes N(2040), N(2700), Delta(2950), exotic states, and states without assigned Monte Carlo numbers.

## Mass selection

- Use `pdgid.data_type = M`, following parent IDs through any nested `SEC` headings to the owning particle. Never infer a mass from arbitrary digits in its name.
- Respect explicit charge magnitude flags. Prefer PDG default properties, then non-alternative properties and PDG order. Units are explicitly converted to MeV; atomic mass units and complex pole strings are not silently interpreted as MeV.
- Prefer numeric PDG summaries. For PDG ranges, use the supplied central value and display the original range in `massNote`; window filtering is on that representative value, not overlap of experimental intervals.
- If no summary record exists, select the first charge-compatible, numeric, non-limit mass measurement in PDG display order. The record and UI identify this as an individual measurement, give its reference, and explicitly state that it is not a PDG average. 133 states currently use this fallback.
- Published limits are never promoted to definite masses or replaced by an unaccepted individual measurement. Unknown/limit-only masses remain null and available in the catalog; they do not establish membership in a numeric energy window. There are 56 entries without a usable numeric mass, including listing topics.
- Every selection retains the property identifier, original unit/value, conversion, source kind, and summary or measurement provenance in `massSelection`. `massPdgIds` links to all mass properties for inspection, including properties not chosen for filtering.

## Quantum numbers

I and J are accepted only as unambiguous integers or half-integers in the explicit `pdgparticle` fields. P and C require an explicit sign. Mixed, missing, or ambiguous values are null. No spin/parity is inferred from resonance names, Monte Carlo IDs, or low star ratings.

Charge is taken from PDG. Additive flavor assignments use the [PDG Monte Carlo numbering scheme](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/reviews/rpp2026-rev-monte-carlo-numbering.pdf) when applicable, otherwise the explicit Listings flavor section or unambiguous heavy/exotic flavor label. The derivation is recorded in `quantumSource`. Unresolved flavor content remains null. Bottomness follows b = -1 and anti-b = +1; consequently B+ has bottomness +1. K-short/K-long are flavor mixtures and do not receive a definite strangeness.

`cParityApplicable = false` distinguishes a state without a C eigenvalue (such as a charged or flavored particle) from an unknown C assignment. Such a state cannot satisfy a requested C eigenvalue; it does not turn pink solely because an unconstrained C value is inapplicable.

## Combination approximation

Additive quantum numbers are summed; an unknown constituent value makes the corresponding total unknown. Angular momenta and isospins use pairwise SU(2) coupling. For a single particle, relative L is inapplicable and cannot change its intrinsic J or P. For combinations, parity is the intrinsic parity product times (-1)^L. For more than two particles, L represents an effective total orbital assignment; independent Jacobi orbitals are not enumerated. Exchange-symmetry selection rules and full dynamical/state constructions are not implemented.

For individually self-conjugate constituents, C is their intrinsic C product, with no extra orbital sign. A neutral particle-antiparticle combination requiring a separate C-eigenstate construction is left unresolved. Results describe compatibility within this model, not a proof of physical existence.
